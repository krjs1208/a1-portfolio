01 / Housing & community

ZOTrent

Finding a place. Finding your people.

A course project exploring rental housing and roommate matching for UCI students, with particular attention to the challenges international students face.

  • Student housing
  • Roommate matching
  • UX design
Explore the ZOTrent project brief

The context

Finding housing also means making decisions about location, cost, and who to live with. International students may be navigating an unfamiliar rental process.

The concept

Bring rental listings and roommate matching into a student-focused experience, with UCI email registration as part of the concept.

A design question

How can an interface help students compare both a place to live and the people they might share it with?

02 / Safety & communication

Safer conversations

A children's chat safety concept.

A design studio project considering children, parents, and moderators as different stakeholders in a safer online chat experience.

  • Interaction design
  • Moderation
  • Mobile UI
Explore the chat safety project brief

The stakeholders

Children need understandable interactions; parents and moderators have different responsibilities when a concern is reported.

The design focus

Explore mobile interfaces, moderation filters, and report details that make the relevant information easier to find.

A design question

How can safety controls feel understandable to a child while giving a moderator enough context to review a report?

03 / AI & play

AI Gaming Companion

Guidance in the flow of play.

A game design project exploring real-time voice guidance as a way to support players without asking them to look away from their game.

  • Voice interaction
  • AI concepts
  • Game experience
Explore the gaming companion project brief

The context

During a game, attention is already divided between the action on screen and the decisions a player needs to make.

The concept

Explore an AI companion that offers voice-based tips in real time, with the experience centered on how help fits into play.

A design question

When is a tip helpful, and when does it become an interruption?

02 / A little about me

People first.
Curiosity always.

I'm Kexin Chen, an undergraduate studying Informatics at the University of California, Irvine. My interests sit at the intersection of human-computer interaction, UX design, and emerging technology.

I'm drawn to everyday problems where a small interaction can make a meaningful difference: finding a roommate, understanding a safety concern, or getting help at the right moment.

I'm also interested in exploring AI, games, and physical interaction as I continue developing my design practice.
